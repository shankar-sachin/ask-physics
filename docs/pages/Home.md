**Ask Physics answers physics questions with answers you can check.** Ask anything from a
textbook problem to "how many rubber ducks would it take to stop a freight train?", and you
get the number, the equation it came from (with its source), every assumption spelled out,
units checked from start to finish, and an honest confidence score.

It runs on your machine, costs nothing, and calls no outside service. The language work is
done by our own small models, the [Fermi models](The-Fermi-Models), trained from scratch. The math
is done by [Noether](Noether), a symbolic math engine built on SymPy and Pint. The models
never do arithmetic.
