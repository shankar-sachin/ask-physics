How it works

Six stages. The language models never do arithmetic: every number comes from Noether (SymPy + Pint).

  1. ClassifyStandard, Fermi estimate, or not physics at all.
  2. RetrieveFind candidate equations in a reviewed database.
  3. PlanPick equations by id and copy the values. No free-form math.
  4. ComputeNoether, our math engine, solves with SymPy and carries units through every step with Pint.
  5. Sanity checkDimensions and orders of magnitude, before you see it.
  6. ExplainPlain words, every assumption listed, confidence scored.
